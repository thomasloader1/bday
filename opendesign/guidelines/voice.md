# Voice

Party suave: warm, direct, Rioplatense. The app talks to friends, not to an audience.

## Rules

- **Language**: Spanish, Rioplatense (Argentina), voseo ("vos", "dale", "fijate", "dejame").
- **Tone**: warm and celebratory, informal but never sloppy. A friend inviting friends.
- **Punctuation**: NO em dashes. Use commas, periods, or parentheses.
- **Exclamations**: party energy is fine ("¡Que bien que vas a venir!"), but not every sentence.
- **Humor**: allowed and encouraged when it fits the party (see "se pico mal", "Alla le vamos a hacer el festejo"), keep it warm, never at a guest's expense.

## Copy conventions

- Buttons: short verbs ("Confirmar", "No Confirmar", "Abrir en Maps", "Agendar en calendario").
- Errors: own the mistake, offer the path forward ("¡Oops! se pico mal. Verifica el número…").
- Never translate app copy to English; code/comments can be English, user-facing text MUST be Spanish.

## Do & Don't

- DO write like you'd text a friend for your birthday.
- DO use voseo consistently ("queres", "podes", "tu presencia").
- DON'T use em dashes; DON'T mix Spanish/English in the same sentence.
- DON'T be formal ("usted") — this is a party.
